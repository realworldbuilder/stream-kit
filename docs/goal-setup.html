Daily sub goal

A bar on your stream that counts today's subs straight from Twitch and starts again at zero every day.

1 Connect Twitch

Twitch asks you to let Stream Kit see your subscribers. That is the only permission it gets: it can't post, change anything, or see your email.

This kit doesn't have a Twitch app yet. You can make your own in 2 minutes:

  1. Go to dev.twitch.tv/console/apps, log in with your Twitch, and click Register Your Application. Twitch may ask you to turn on two-factor login first.
  2. Name: Stream Kit plus your name (it has to be unique). OAuth Redirect URL: . Category: Broadcasting Suite. Client Type: Confidential. Then click Create.

3 Put it in OBS

  1. Copy your link: Copied

    This link has your Twitch login inside it. Don't show it on stream or send it to anyone.

  2. In OBS, under Sources click + → Browser → name it "Sub goal" → OK.
  3. Paste the link into URL. Set Width 600, Height 120. Click OK, then drag it where you want it.

Good to know

  • Wrong number? Right-click the Sub goal source → Interact, then press + or - to fix it, or 0 to reset.
  • It only counts subs while OBS is open. Subs that came in while OBS was closed won't show; add them with +.
  • If the bar ever says the login ran out, come back here, connect again, and paste the new link into the same source (double-click it in OBS).
